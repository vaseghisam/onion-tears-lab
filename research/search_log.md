# Focused literature search log

Date: 24 September 2026. Search service: two public web-search indexes, with direct opens of publisher pages, PubMed/PMC records, and author-institution repositories. Primary research was required for scientific claims. Routine repeats and formatting variants are not enumerated below. This is a reproducible record of the query families and decisions, not a complete database export or a systematic-review flow diagram.

## Scope

Include original onion LF chemistry, original human exposure studies, analytical time courses, controlled cutting mechanics, and original remedy or low-LF cultivar comparisons. Prefer the peer-reviewed version when a preprint has a later publication. Exclude popular explanations as scientific evidence, unrelated Allium species as direct evidence for bulb-onion remedies, and studies of non-LF irritants as direct LF evidence.

The root/folklore/goggles/water searches use “not found in this search,” not “no study exists.” A proper systematic efficacy review would require more databases, explicit date/language criteria, duplicate screening, and access to all eligible methods.

## Retrieval limitations

PMC intermittently returned an automated browser check; Nature sometimes redirected to a cookie endpoint; ACS sometimes returned 403; MDPI returned 429 or an inaccessible-page response. Retrieved text was read when available. No subscription purchase, credential request, access-control bypass, or third-party full-paper copying was performed.

Imai2002: indexed publisher abstract. Eady2008: main full text available. Silvaroli2017: PubMed abstract and figure captions; main text not retrieved. Higashihara2010: publisher abstract only. Wu2025: published main full text; data and supplement linked but not downloaded or reanalyzed. Kato2016: main full text and methods, through a publisher URL returned by the search service. Lokke2012: indexed publisher abstract plus author-institution metadata. Joyce2013: bibliographic record only for adoption purposes. Arakawa2020: author-institution abstract and metadata.

## Provenance and version control

Wu2025 was accepted 6 September 2025, published online 7 October 2025, and assigned to the 21 October 2025 issue. This status precedes the review date. The May 2025 arXiv version is not cited as the sole authority. Publisher dates take precedence over the search index's sometimes inconsistent “years ago” labels or a dynamically rendered preprint date.

No source paper or experimental footage is bundled. Readers can follow the DOI links, PubMed/PMC entries, and the Wu study's OSF deposit. All project-generated calculations are identified separately from published experimental evidence.

## Preliminary patent evidence

[EP2014333A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP2014333A1/en), published 14 January 2009, describes a 10-adult onion-grating test of cooling-agent formulations, including menthol gum versus gum without menthol (Test Examples, Test 1, Table 1). It reports mean onset times but gives no participant-level results or variability, no adequate randomization/carryover account, and no demonstrated independent replication. It is a patent report, not a peer-reviewed efficacy study. This does not validate ordinary gum or justify a numerical protection claim. Accordingly, “no adequate peer-reviewed evidence located” is used for the article’s general gum advice; an absolute claim that no controlled comparison exists would be inaccurate.
