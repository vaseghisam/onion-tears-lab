# Evidence review

Review date: 24 September 2026. Source keys below match `references.bib` and `claim_ledger.csv`. `reference_crosswalk.csv` maps the eight article reference numbers to those keys.

This is a focused primary-source review for an explanatory article, not a preregistered systematic review or meta-analysis. The search history and retrieval limits appear in `search_log.md`. A remedy described as unestablished has no adequate controlled comparison in the material retrieved; that statement does not prove that the remedy has no effect.

## What the article can establish

The reaction pathway, LF's capacity to elicit human irritation and reflex tearing, and the existence of both vapor emissions and liquid spray have direct experimental support. Quantitative efficacy for a complete household preparation task is less well established. The computational part therefore uses a stated hypothetical source and transport conditions. It does not calibrate chemical production, goggle performance, or a human tear threshold.

## Chemistry and the eye

The checked scheme is isoalliin, acted on by alliinase, to 1-propenesulfenic acid; LF synthase catalyzes formation of syn-propanethial S-oxide, also described as (Z)-propanethial S-oxide. The sulfenic-acid intermediate can instead enter thiosulfinate chemistry. Enzymes should label reaction arrows; neither enzyme is the irritant emitted as a molecular vapor. The diagram is a pathway schematic, not a balanced account of every coproduct. Sources: Eady2008 Fig. 1 and Silvaroli2017 Fig. 1.

Higashihara2010 exposed one eye of each of 91 volunteers to synthesized LF and assessed irritation-tolerance time and change in tear-meniscus radius. Its abstract supports a direct ocular reflex response and age-related differences. It supplies neither a general airborne kitchen threshold nor a conversion from modeled exposure to tear volume. The complete protocol was inaccessible, so no exposure concentration is inferred.

No retrieved primary experiment established purified onion LF activation of a named TRPA1 receptor. Findings for garlic allicin, mustard compounds, or mixed extracts do not fill that gap. The article need not specify a receptor. The familiar sulfuric-acid-in-the-eye explanation is not supported by the retrieved chemistry and is omitted; no alternative ocular reaction is invented.

## Reading the recent cutting experiment correctly

Wu2025 is a peer-reviewed article published online 7 October 2025, not only a May 2025 preprint. Consult the two concise ledger entries for its experimental findings and limitations. The original authors' [OSF deposit](https://osf.io/5xady/) contains data and MATLAB code. This project does not redistribute or reanalyze those files and does not reproduce their experimental footage. Our gas-transport animation is an independent conditional calculation.

## Practical evidence decisions

| Method | Evidence category in this project | Limit on recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goggles that seal around the eyes | Physical barrier inference | No controlled onion-specific efficacy estimate was located; fit and leakage matter. Do not assign a protection percentage. |
| Air carried from the cutting area away from the face | Transport reasoning; conditional project calculation | Direction and geometry matter. A fan and an extracting hood are not interchangeable flow fields. No household trial is claimed. |
| Sharp blade and gentle cuts | Direct mechanical evidence | See Wu2025 ledger entry; do not translate spray changes into a tear-reduction percentage. |
| Chilling | Possible chemical and mechanical changes | See Wu2025 temperature entry. Net whole-task irritation remains unresolved in the retrieved evidence. |
| Water in direct contact with cut tissue | Possible transfer into liquid and removal from the source | Not established as a complete prevention method here. Nearby water is a different intervention, with no mechanism that attracts all passing vapor. |
| A variety shown to produce little LF | Cultivar-specific chemical and sensory evidence | Kato2016 involved experimental lines and chewing; it is not a standardized cutting trial or a commercial-product guarantee. |
| Leaving the root end attached | Prevention unestablished | No controlled LF or tear comparison was found. Root sulfur claims and results from other Allium species do not establish this kitchen remedy. |
| Bread, gum, an unlit match, or a candle | Prevention unestablished | No adequate peer-reviewed benefit was located. A preliminary formulated-gum patent is logged below. |

The reasonable practical preference for a physical barrier or air transport away from the face is an inference from the exposure route. It is not presented as the winner of a clinical comparison. A wet towel, an open bowl of water, rinsing cut onions, and cutting under water must not share one evidence label merely because all involve water.

## Sources that alter the interpretation

Kato2016 reported low LF production in two selected lines. Its 20-person sensory panel chewed fresh tissue and rated tear-inducing sensation and pungency. Several authors worked for House Foods or a plant breeder; the paper declares no competing financial interests. These details matter if the article discusses flavor or uses the word “tearless.” Eady2008 also includes House Foods researchers; its gene-silencing experiment targets LFS, whereas Kato2016 reduced alliinase activity. The interventions must not be merged.

Lokke2012 monitored headspace composition over two hours, with LF-related signals prominent early. Its observations establish changing chemistry. A normalized release kernel in this project remains an assumed source history, not a fit to those data. Total volatile-organic-compound signal, smell, liquid spray volume, and LF concentration are different endpoints.

A patent report, Mirkheshti2009, describes a small controlled comparison involving a formulated menthol gum. Its reporting is insufficient for household guidance; the search log gives the exclusion rationale. This is why the article qualifies the evidence search and does not assert that no controlled comparison has ever been attempted.

## Unresolved measurements

No LF-specific gas diffusivity, Henry coefficient, environmental first-order loss constant, chilled-onion emission factor, goggle leakage fraction, or universal tear threshold was obtained from the checked sources. Any value used for those quantities in the computational package must be labeled a scenario assumption, justified separately, or omitted. A simulated decrease in concentration at one observation location cannot by itself quantify how much a person's tears decrease.

Exact claim locations, decision status, and measurement endpoints are recorded in `claim_ledger.csv`. Bibliography entries do not imply that a full paper was accessible or that every claim in it was adopted.
